Minimalist launcher: style options

Each option shows two phone screens at 390px wide: the home screen, and the screen after you long-press an app. Camera is pinned first, and the rest of the apps follow in alphabetical order. No icons, wallpaper, widgets or app drawer. Each screen scrolls, so you can drag or mouse-wheel inside it.

A: Phosphor

Old CRT terminal. Lowercase names, green accent #39ff14 on near-black #050605. A long-press highlights the row and shows the action on a line underneath it.

B: Synthwave

Spaced-out uppercase names with faint index numbers. Magenta accent #ff2a6d with a soft glow on violet-black #0a0710. A long-press adds a neon bar on the left of the row and a chip on the right.

C: Ice

Larger type with more space around it. Cyan accent #00e5ff on pure black, with very faint scanlines. A long-press dims the rest of the list, gives the chosen app a slight glitch effect and slides a bar up from the bottom.

Same in every option

  • The status bar (time and battery) belongs to Android. The launcher keeps it dark to match.
  • For system apps that can't be uninstalled, the action reads "app info" and opens Android's App Info screen.
  • The mockup font is JetBrains Mono. On the phone we can bundle it (about 100 KB) or use Android's built-in monospace font, which looks plainer.
  • If you'd rather have no in-list action, a long-press could open the system uninstall dialog straight away. That is one less tap, but there is no confirmation step inside the launcher.
